# Research and Public Project Dependency Map

These are projects or ecosystems Zero-Mass agents should study before rebuilding equivalent functionality.

## Bayesian/UQ/model mixing

- BAND framework: https://github.com/bandframework/bandframework
- Bayesian Mass Explorer data/code: https://github.com/massexplorer/bmex-masses
- BUQEYE: https://buqeye.github.io/
- BUQEYE emulator review code: https://github.com/buqeye/frontiers-emulator-review
- GSUM: https://github.com/buqeye/gsum

## Nuclear landscape emulation and interpretable ML

- BANNANE reproduction repository: https://github.com/munozariasjm/paper_o_bannane
- Nuclear MISR: https://github.com/munozariasjm/nuclear-misr
- Nuclear DNA: https://github.com/strifinopoulos/Nuclear_DNA

## High-fidelity nuclear calculation ecosystems

- NUCLEI SciDAC: https://nuclei.mps.ohio-state.edu/
- NuclearToolkit.jl: https://github.com/sotayoshida/NuclearToolkit.jl
- imsrg++: https://github.com/ragnarstroberg/imsrg
- NuHamil: https://github.com/Takayuki-Miyagi/NuHamil-public

## Nuclear data

- Atomic Mass Data Center: https://www-nds.iaea.org/amdc/
- NNDC: https://www.nndc.bnl.gov/

## Architectural rule

Use these projects as scientific and engineering references. Do not vendor or copy third-party code into Zero-Mass without reviewing its license and dependency implications.
